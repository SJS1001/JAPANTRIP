# Tokyo + Hakone restaurant audit

**Snapshot:** 2026-07-27  
**Scope:** the existing Tokyo/Hakone `areaKey` values in `data/card-guides.ts`. Choices are ranked for the scheduled attraction, a family of four, August heat, and realistic walking/transit—not as a citywide awards list.

## Method and safety legend

- Ratings are dated snapshots. A number is shown only when a current Tabelog page exposed both the score and review count; otherwise it is `—`. Tabelog is conservative, so a 3.5 score with substantial volume is generally strong.
- `dedicated gluten-free`: official source says the menu/kitchen excludes wheat, barley and rye. Production-source traces may still be possible; confirm the family's required safety level.
- `gluten-free-menu`: labeled GF dishes exist, but the venue uses a mixed kitchen.
- `accommodates-request`: the venue says it can adapt with advance contact; controls are not described as dedicated.
- `uncertain`: no current official gluten-control claim was verified. This is **not** a safe celiac recommendation.
- Ordinary soy sauce, eel sauce, curry roux, tempura, soba, udon, ramen, breading and shared fryers commonly contain wheat.

## Critical corrections for the calendar

1. **Remove Tokyo Shiba Tofuya Ukai.** Its current Tabelog page says it permanently closed at the end of March 2026. The historical 3.66/1,765 score must not be presented as a live choice. [Closure evidence](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1314/A131401/13019665/)
2. **Do not show Tsunahachi Sohonten as open.** The Shinjuku main-store listing is on hold after the location closed May 31, 2026 for rebuilding. Use Tsunahachi Keio instead. [Current main-store status](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1304/A130401/13000859/) · [Keio branch](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1304/A130401/13017310/)
3. **Sometaro does not take reservations July–September.** It remains a good Asakusa/Kappabashi option, but only as a walk-in queue. [Current listing](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1311/A131102/13003710/)
4. **Hakone mountain stops need carried food for strict gluten avoidance.** No current official celiac-control statement was found for the Open-Air Museum café, Sounzan or Owakudani. The museum asks guests to discuss allergies; that is not a GF guarantee. [Museum café](https://www.hakone-oam.or.jp/en/restaurantsandshops/cafe)
5. **Odawara Oden Honten is not buffer-friendly.** It is a good restaurant but about an 18-minute walk from Odawara Station. For the protected 18:07 Shinkansen, use station-adjacent Oden Honjin/Uosei instead.
